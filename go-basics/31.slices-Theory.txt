  - Slice :  it is defined as the contiguous segment of underlying array. 
   and it provides to a numbered sequence of elements from that array.
   so as such, slices provides access to parts parts of an array in sequential order

  - slices are more felxible and more powerful than arrays, since array had a limitation that are of fixed type

    due to this, it offers more flexibility than arrays.

  The Rule of copy
The built-in copy(destination, source) function does something very specific: it returns the number of elements it actually copied.

To decide how many elements to copy, Go looks at the lengths of both slices and takes the smaller length:

Length of source (arr) = 5

Length of destination (slice) = 10

Since the smaller length is 5, Go can only copy 5 elements over. Even though your destination slice had room for 10, the source slice ran out of items after 5.
